Live sync lets everyone's phone see the same scores in real time. It's free and takes ~5 minutes. Follow the steps:

1
Go to console.firebase.google.com, sign in with a Google account and click "Create a project". Name it anything (e.g. "city-challenge"). You can turn Analytics off.
2
In the left menu open Build → Realtime Database and click "Create Database". Pick the location closest to you.
3
When asked about security rules, choose "Start in test mode". (Test mode lets phones read & write without logins — perfect for a one-day event.)
4
Click the ⚙️ gear → Project settings, scroll to "Your apps", click the </> (Web) icon, give it any nickname and click "Register app". Copy the firebaseConfig object shown.
5
Paste it below. Make sure it contains databaseURL — that's the address of your live database, the app can't sync without it. If it's missing, copy the URL shown at the top of the Realtime Database page (looks like https://xxx.firebasedatabase.app) and add it as "databaseURL".
